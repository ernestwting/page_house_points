Scoring codex

  1. Net power = ((Points + Beer Room Boost + 15 for 15) × Shot O'Clock) + Polar Bear + Whose House + Happy Hour. Polar Bear, Whose House, and Happy Hour can each be positive or negative — Polar Bear is meant as a penalty, so its preset applies a negative amount by default.
  2. Tier is assigned automatically by rank: top 10% is Ω Apex, next 20% Σ Prime, next 30% Δ Flux, next 25% λ Drift, bottom 15% ∅ Null.
  3. Every category below is admin-adjustable per person — click a boost preset, pick who it applies to, and it adds (or, with a negative amount, subtracts) from that category.
  4. Click any leaderboard row for that Pageboy's full formula breakdown.
